"Font Feelings" Presentation

Think you know when to choose the right font? This presentation will challenge everything you know about typography and dive deeper into how different fonts can influence our perceptions of emotions, sounds, and even taste!

I based this presentation around the incredible research by Sarah Hyndman who studies the effects typography have on perception. I designed this presentation to be informative, interactive, and approachable for designers and non-designers alike.
