Incorporating AI in the Design Process

The landscape for design-friendly AI tools is constantly evolving and improving and it’s up to us design leaders to help guide our teams through this ever-changing landscape. Here are just some of the ways I utilize AI in my design workflows and how I guide product teams and designers to leverage AI tools in their everyday tasks.

Product Strategy and Brainstorming:

  • Generate a GANNT chart based on a PRD
  • Organize FigJam sticky notes based on central themes
  • Summarize a FigJam session for stakeholders
  • Write product support documentation for users
  • Identify flaws and user pain points in a PRD, design concept, or current state
  • List out user stories to ensure all use cases are accounted for

Prototyping and Ideation:

  • Get design crit feedback to make actionable edits
  • Feed in a design and generate alternative layouts and theming
  • Rapid prototyping to test early concepts
  • Simplify an existing design into wires for stakeholder focus

Hi-Fidelity Design Work:

  • Demonstrate micro-animations and user interactions for both engineers and stakeholders
  • Write concise microcopy for UIs
  • Run an accessibility audit using WCAG standards

Usability Testing and Measuring Success:

  • Summarize UX research into actionable suggestions
  • Generate survey/interview questions for users
  • Connect Claude to HotJar/GA to gain insights into user behaviors and UI performance
  • Analyze trends and results of usability research

Tooling I Use:

  • Figma Make
  • Claude Code
  • Gemini
  • Loveable
  • Moonchild AI
