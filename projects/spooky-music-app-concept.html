Spooky Music App Concept

This was such a fun design challenge! For this brief, I created a music app that incorporates spooky Halloween-themed iconography. In the UI, you’ll find playful versions of common icons found in music apps, including Play, Favorites, Profile, and more!

When creating these icons, I wanted them to be recognizable, while still being slightly surprising and charming. My typical illustration style embraces slightly imperfect lines and I tried to bring that in here as well.


If you look closely, you’ll find corners that are intentionally asymmetrical and lines that are ever so slightly askew to capture the hand-drawn look of my other illustrations.


🎃 Note: My favorite icon is the Trick-or-Treat bag (Shopping Bag).

Access the file on Figma.

I wanted to incorporate colors that felt “spooky” and evoked Halloween themes without being too on-the-nose. I came up with this vibrant, 80s-inspired palette that ties nicely into the music theme of the app.
