The brief

Willow & Rye had outgrown the DIY site they'd built when they opened. Custom cake orders were coming in through a mix of Instagram DMs and voicemail, and nothing about the site matched the warmth of the actual bakery.

What we built

We rebuilt the whole thing in Squarespace, centered on a custom order form that captures flavor, size, and pickup date up front — no back-and-forth required. The visual identity leans into the same soft, buttery palette as their packaging.

The result

Custom orders now come in pre-organized by pickup date, and the owner can update seasonal flavors herself in about five minutes, no help required.
