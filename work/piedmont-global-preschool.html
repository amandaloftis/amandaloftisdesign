The brief

Piedmont Global was opening a second campus and needed a site that could handle bilingual enrollment without turning into a wall of PDFs.

What we built

A bilingual Framer site with a guided enrollment flow that walks families through campus choice, age group and language track before anyone has to pick up the phone.

The result

Both campuses now share one enrollment pipeline, and staff spend less time re-entering the same information by hand.
