Ridgeline Trail Co.

Hand-drawn trail maps and a merch line for a regional outfitter.

IMAGE
Trail map
Merch line

The brief

A regional outfitter wanted trail maps and merch that felt hand-drawn and specific to their routes, not stock topographic clip art.

What we built

A set of hand-drawn trail maps and a small merch line — patches, stickers and a tee — that all pull from the same illustration set.

The result

The trail maps now hang in-store and ship inside every online order as a printed insert.
