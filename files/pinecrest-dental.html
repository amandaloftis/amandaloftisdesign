The brief

Pinecrest's old site buried the booking link three clicks deep, and the design felt more like a hospital than a neighborhood practice that mostly sees families.

What we built

We simplified the whole structure down to four pages, built a booking flow that surfaces the right appointment type immediately, and softened the whole visual tone with warmer colors and friendlier photography placement.

The result

New patient bookings made it through the online form instead of stalling out on the phone — and the front desk stopped fielding "where do I click" calls.
